**Engineering Lead, Dispatch Data**
Kestrel Freight Network · Remote (EU) · Posted 31 March 2026

Kestrel plans and settles cross-border road freight for about 900 shippers. Dispatch Data is
the group that turns the day's confirmed loads into the run sheets every depot works from. The
build runs overnight and depots have it by 05:00 local; when it is late, trucks wait.

We are hiring an Engineering Lead to run that group.

**What you'll do**

- Own the reliability of the overnight dispatch build against a contractual 05:00 hand-off.
- Lead the move of the batch workloads onto our event-streaming backbone, without a
  customer-visible break in service.
- Line-manage a team of four to six engineers — hiring, growth, performance, the lot.
- Take on an on-call rotation the group currently dreads and make it something people will
  stay for.
- Set technical direction with the platform and depot-systems groups, neither of which you
  will own.
- Operate the group's Kubernetes footprint alongside the platform team.

**What we're looking for**

- 8+ years building and running production backend systems.
- Demonstrable experience line-managing engineers, including performance and growth
  conversations you personally held.
- Deep production Kafka experience — you have moved a real workload onto it, not read about it.
- Comfort setting direction across teams you have no authority over.
- Strong SQL and Postgres. Airflow or equivalent.
- A degree in a technical field or equivalent experience.
- A genuine passion for logistics.
- Thrives in ambiguity and wears many hats.
